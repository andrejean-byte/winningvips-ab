Bonuses & Promotions

What are wagering requirements?

Wagering requirements (also called playthrough requirements) specify how many times you must bet through a bonus amount before you can withdraw the winnings. For example, a $100 bonus with 30x wagering means you need to place a total of $3,000 in bets before cashing out.

Lower wagering requirements are better for players. Some bonuses also have game weighting, meaning certain games contribute less toward meeting the requirement. For a full explanation, see our Understanding Casino Bonuses guide. You can also look up "wagering requirements" in our Casino Glossary.

Can I claim bonuses from multiple casinos?

Yes. There is nothing wrong with signing up at multiple casinos and claiming their welcome bonuses. In fact, this is a common strategy among experienced players to maximize value. Each casino's bonus is independent and subject to its own terms and conditions.

Just make sure you read and understand the wagering requirements and bonus terms at each casino before claiming.

Do bonuses expire?

Yes, most casino bonuses have an expiration date. Typical timeframes range from 7 to 30 days after claiming. If you do not meet the wagering requirements within that period, the bonus and any associated winnings may be forfeited.

Always check the bonus terms for the specific expiry period before claiming. We highlight these details in all of our casino reviews.

What is a no deposit bonus?

A no deposit bonus is a promotion that gives you free bonus funds or free spins without requiring you to deposit any money. These bonuses are typically small (e.g., $10 or 20 free spins) and come with strict wagering requirements and maximum withdrawal limits.

Deposits & Withdrawals

What payment methods are accepted?

Most online casinos accept a wide range of payment methods, including:

  • Bank transfers and wire transfers
  • Prepaid cards (Paysafecard, Neosurf)
  • Cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, USDT, and more)

Available methods vary by casino and by your country. See our Payment Methods Guide for a detailed comparison.

How fast are withdrawals?

Withdrawal speed depends on the payment method and the casino's processing time:

  • Cryptocurrency: Typically 10 minutes to a few hours
  • E-wallets: Usually 0-24 hours
  • Credit/debit cards: 1-5 business days
  • Bank transfers: 3-7 business days

Many casinos also have a "pending period" (24-72 hours) before processing begins. The casinos we rate highest tend to have the fastest payouts. We test withdrawals firsthand for every casino we review.

Are there deposit or withdrawal fees?

Most reputable casinos do not charge fees for deposits. Some may charge a small fee for certain withdrawal methods or for withdrawals below a minimum threshold. Cryptocurrency transactions may involve blockchain network fees, which are not controlled by the casino.

We always note any fees in our casino reviews. As a general rule, the best casinos offer at least one fee-free withdrawal method.

Is cryptocurrency accepted at online casinos?

Yes, and crypto is increasingly popular. Many of the casinos we review accept Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Litecoin (LTC), Tether (USDT), and other cryptocurrencies. Crypto deposits and withdrawals are typically faster, have lower fees, and offer greater privacy than traditional methods.

Account & Security

How do I create a casino account?

Creating an account at most online casinos takes just a few minutes:

  • Visit the casino's website and click the "Sign Up" or "Register" button
  • Enter your personal details (name, email, date of birth, country)
  • Choose a username and strong password
  • Verify your email address
  • Complete KYC verification when required (usually before your first withdrawal)

Our Beginner's Guide walks through the process step by step.

What is KYC verification?

KYC stands for "Know Your Customer." It is a legal requirement at licensed casinos to verify your identity and prevent fraud, money laundering, and underage gambling. The KYC process typically requires you to submit:

  • A government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID card)
  • Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or official letter dated within the last 3 months)
  • Proof of payment method (photo of card used or e-wallet screenshot)

Completing KYC early speeds up your first withdrawal. Learn more in our Casino Glossary under "KYC."

How do I self-exclude from a casino?

All licensed casinos are required to offer self-exclusion options. You can typically self-exclude by:

  • Navigating to the "Responsible Gambling" section in your account settings
  • Choosing a self-exclusion period (e.g., 24 hours, 7 days, 1 month, 6 months, or permanently)
  • Contacting customer support directly to request account closure

During self-exclusion, you cannot log in, deposit, or place bets. For more tools and resources, visit our Responsible Gambling page.

Is my personal data safe at online casinos?

Reputable, licensed casinos use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption (128-bit or 256-bit) for all data transmissions
  • Secure payment processing through certified providers
  • Data protection policies compliant with GDPR and other regulations
  • Regular security audits by independent firms

We verify security practices as part of our review process. Only casinos that meet our security standards make it onto our recommended list.

Should I enable two-factor authentication?

Absolutely. If a casino offers two-factor authentication (2FA), we strongly recommend enabling it. 2FA adds an extra layer of security by requiring a second verification step (usually a code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app) when logging in. This significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access to your account.
