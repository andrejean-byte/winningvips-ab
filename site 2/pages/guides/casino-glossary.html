Online Casino Glossary
80+ Terms Explained

From basic betting terms to advanced gambling jargon, this comprehensive glossary covers every term you will encounter at online casinos.

ABCD EFGH IJKL MNOPQ RSTU VW

A

Action
The total amount of money wagered by a player during a session or over a specific period. Used by casinos to track betting volume.
Ante
A mandatory bet placed before cards are dealt, commonly used in poker and some table games. The ante ensures there is always money in the pot.
Autoplay
A slot machine feature that allows you to set the game to spin automatically for a selected number of rounds without pressing the spin button each time.

B

Baccarat
A card game where the goal is to bet on whether the player's or banker's hand will be closer to 9. One of the most popular table games at both land-based and online casinos, known for its low house edge.
Bankroll
The total amount of money a player has set aside specifically for gambling. Proper bankroll management is essential for responsible play.
Bet Max
A button on slot machines that places the maximum allowed wager per spin. Some progressive jackpots require a max bet to qualify for the top prize.
Blackjack
A card game where the goal is to get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. One of the most popular casino games with a low house edge.
Bonus
An incentive offered by a casino to attract or reward players. Common types include welcome bonuses, reload bonuses, free spins, and cashback offers. Always check the wagering requirements attached.
Bonus Abuse
Deliberately exploiting casino bonuses in ways that violate the terms of service. Casinos actively monitor for bonus abuse and may void winnings or close accounts.
Bust
In blackjack, going over 21. When a player busts, they lose their bet regardless of the dealer's hand.

C

Cashback
A bonus that returns a percentage of a player's net losses over a specified period. Cashback offers typically range from 5% to 20% and may have lower wagering requirements than standard bonuses.
Casino Hold
The percentage of all wagers the casino retains as profit over time. The inverse of the return to player (RTP).
Coin Value
The denomination of a single coin in a slot game. Adjusting the coin value changes the total bet per spin.
Comp Points
Loyalty points earned through real-money play that can be exchanged for bonuses, free spins, or cash. Most VIP programs are built around comp point systems.
Croupier
The casino employee who manages a table game, dealing cards, spinning the roulette wheel, and paying out winners. In online live dealer games, the croupier is streamed via video.
Cryptocurrency Casino
An online casino that accepts Bitcoin, Ethereum, or other digital currencies for deposits and withdrawals. Crypto casinos often offer faster payouts and enhanced privacy.

D

Deposit
Transferring real money from your bank account, card, e-wallet, or crypto wallet into your casino account. This funds your balance so you can place bets.
Deposit Limit
A responsible gambling tool that lets players set a maximum amount they can deposit within a day, week, or month. Helps prevent overspending.
Double Down
In blackjack, doubling your initial bet after receiving your first two cards and receiving exactly one more card. A strategic move when you have a favorable hand.
Drop
The total amount of money exchanged for chips at a table game. Used by casinos as a measure of table activity.

E

Edge (House Edge)
The mathematical advantage the casino has over the player on any given game, expressed as a percentage. For example, a 2% house edge means the casino expects to keep $2 for every $100 wagered over time.
Even Money
A bet that pays 1:1 if you win, meaning you receive the same amount as your wager. Red/black bets in roulette are an example.
Expected Value (EV)
A mathematical calculation of the average amount a player can expect to win or lose per bet over the long run. Positive EV (+EV) means the bet favors the player; negative EV (-EV) favors the casino.

F

Fixed Jackpot
A jackpot with a predetermined payout amount that does not change regardless of how many players play the game. Contrast with progressive jackpots.
Flat Betting
A betting strategy where the player wagers the same amount on every bet, regardless of wins or losses. Considered the safest approach to bankroll management.
Free Spins
Bonus rounds on slot games where you spin the reels without wagering your own money. Free spins can be awarded as part of a welcome bonus, in-game feature, or ongoing promotion.
Fruit Machine
A traditional term for slot machines, originating from the UK where early machines displayed fruit symbols on the reels.

G

Gambler's Fallacy
The mistaken belief that past results influence future outcomes in games of chance. For example, believing that a roulette wheel is "due" to land on red after several consecutive blacks. Each spin is independent.
Game Weighting
The percentage that different games contribute toward meeting wagering requirements. Slots typically count 100%, while table games may count only 10-20%.
GGR (Gross Gaming Revenue)
The total amount wagered by players minus the total winnings paid out. This is the primary revenue metric for casinos.

H

High Roller
A player who consistently wagers large amounts of money. High rollers (also called whales) often receive special VIP treatment, including personal account managers, higher limits, and exclusive bonuses.
Hit
In blackjack, requesting an additional card from the dealer. A player hits when they want to increase their hand total.
Hold Percentage
The percentage of total bets a casino keeps as revenue from a particular game or table over a period. Similar to house edge but measured from actual results rather than theoretical math.

I

In-Play Betting
Placing bets on a sporting event or game that is currently in progress. Also called live betting, odds are updated in real time based on the action.
Insurance
A side bet in blackjack offered when the dealer's upcard is an ace. It pays 2:1 if the dealer has blackjack. Statistically, insurance is generally a losing bet.

J

Jackpot
The largest possible payout on a casino game. Jackpots can be fixed (a set amount) or progressive (growing with every bet placed until someone wins).
Juice (Vig)
The commission or fee charged by the house, especially in sports betting. Also known as vigorish. Typically around 10% on standard bets.

K

KYC (Know Your Customer)
Identity verification required by licensed casinos before processing withdrawals. KYC typically involves submitting a government-issued ID, proof of address, and sometimes proof of payment method. This is a legal requirement to prevent fraud and money laundering.

L

Live Dealer
Online casino games hosted by real human dealers streamed via video in real time. Popular live dealer games include blackjack, roulette, baccarat, and game shows. They bridge the gap between online and land-based casinos.
Loss Limit
A responsible gambling feature that caps the amount a player can lose in a given timeframe. Once the limit is reached, the player cannot place further bets until the period resets.
Lobby
The main page of an online casino where players browse and select games. Lobbies are typically organized by category (slots, table games, live dealer, jackpots) and may include search and filter features.
Low Roller
A player who bets small amounts. Many online casinos cater to low rollers with low minimum bets (sometimes as low as $0.10 per spin).

M

Martingale
A betting strategy where the player doubles their bet after every loss, with the theory that a single win will recover all previous losses. While mathematically sound in theory, the Martingale system is risky because table limits and bankroll constraints can result in significant losses.
Max Bet
The highest amount a player can wager on a single spin or hand. When playing with bonus funds, casinos often impose a lower max bet (e.g., $5) to prevent large bets with bonus money.
Multiplier
A feature in slot games that multiplies winnings by a specified factor (2x, 3x, 10x, etc.). Multipliers can appear during base play, free spins, or bonus rounds.

N

No Deposit Bonus
A casino bonus that does not require the player to make a deposit. Typically small amounts (e.g., $10 or 20 free spins), these bonuses let new players try a casino risk-free but usually come with strict wagering requirements and withdrawal caps.
Natural
In blackjack, a hand totaling 21 with the first two cards (an ace plus a 10-value card). Also called a "blackjack," it usually pays 3:2.

O

Odds
The probability of a particular outcome occurring, usually expressed as a ratio. Odds determine the payout for a winning bet. In casino games, the odds are always set to favor the house over the long term.
Over/Under
A sports betting wager on whether the total points scored in a game will be above or below a specified number set by the bookmaker.

Q

Qualifier
In poker, the minimum hand required to be eligible for a payout. In Caribbean Stud Poker, for example, the dealer must have at least an ace-king to qualify.

P

Payline
A line across the reels of a slot machine on which matching symbols must land to produce a win. Classic slots have a single payline; modern video slots can have 10, 25, 50, or even hundreds of paylines.
Payout
The amount of money returned to the player for a winning bet. Also used to refer to the overall percentage of money returned to players (see RTP).
Paytable
A chart within a slot or table game that shows all possible winning combinations and their corresponding payouts. Always review the paytable before playing a new game.
Progressive Jackpot
A jackpot that grows incrementally as players place bets. A small percentage of each wager is added to the prize pool. Progressive jackpots can reach millions of dollars and are often linked across multiple casinos.
Provably Fair
A cryptographic system used by some crypto casinos that allows players to independently verify the fairness of each game result. Uses hash seeds that can be checked after each round.

R

Rake
The commission fee taken by the casino from each poker hand or tournament buy-in. The rake is the primary way poker rooms generate revenue.
Reload Bonus
A bonus offered to existing players when they make subsequent deposits (not their first). Reload bonuses are typically smaller than welcome bonuses but can be claimed repeatedly.
Reels
The vertical columns on a slot machine that spin and display symbols. Traditional slots have 3 reels, while modern video slots typically have 5 or more. Some innovative slots feature expanding or cascading reel mechanics.
RNG (Random Number Generator)
A software algorithm that produces random outcomes in online casino games, ensuring every spin, card deal, or dice roll is independent and unpredictable. Licensed casinos use certified RNG systems audited by third parties like eCOGRA or iTech Labs.
RTP (Return to Player)
The theoretical percentage of all wagered money a game will pay back to players over time. For example, a slot with 96% RTP will return $96 for every $100 wagered on average. Higher RTP means a lower house edge.

S

Scatter
A special symbol in slot games that typically triggers bonus features (such as free spins) regardless of its position on the reels. Unlike regular symbols, scatters do not need to land on a specific payline.
Self-Exclusion
A responsible gambling tool that allows players to voluntarily ban themselves from a casino for a set period (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, or permanently). During self-exclusion, the player cannot access their account or place bets.
Side Bet
An optional additional wager placed alongside the main bet in table games. Side bets usually offer higher payouts but come with a significantly higher house edge.
Slot Volatility (Variance)
A measure of risk in a slot game. Low volatility slots pay out small wins frequently, while high volatility slots pay larger amounts less often. Medium volatility offers a balance between the two.
Soft Hand
In blackjack, a hand containing an ace that can be counted as either 1 or 11 without busting. For example, an ace and a 6 is a "soft 17."
Split
In blackjack, dividing a pair of matching cards into two separate hands, each with its own bet. This doubles the player's wager but creates two chances to beat the dealer.
Sticky Bonus
A bonus that cannot be withdrawn itself but can be used to place bets. Only the winnings from the bonus can be cashed out after meeting wagering requirements. Also called a non-cashable bonus.
Surrender
A blackjack option that allows a player to forfeit half their bet and end the hand immediately. Available in some game variants as either early or late surrender.

T

Table Limit
The minimum and maximum bet amounts allowed at a casino table game. Online casinos typically offer a wide range of table limits to accommodate both low rollers and high rollers.
Tilt
An emotional state where a player makes irrational decisions, often after a bad beat or a losing streak. Playing on tilt leads to poor bankroll management and bigger losses.
Tournament
A competitive event where players compete against each other on specific games (usually slots or poker) for a prize pool. Leaderboard standings are based on points, wins, or total wagered.
True Odds
The actual mathematical probability of an event occurring, before the house edge is applied. Casino payout odds are always slightly lower than true odds, which is how the house makes a profit.
Turbo Mode
A slot feature that speeds up the reel spinning animation, allowing faster play. Some experienced players prefer turbo mode for efficiency, though it can lead to faster bankroll depletion.

U

Upcards
In blackjack, the dealer's face-up card that is visible to all players before they make their decisions. Basic strategy charts are based on the dealer's upcard.

V

VIP Program
A loyalty rewards system that offers exclusive benefits to frequent or high-spending players. VIP perks typically include higher withdrawal limits, personal account managers, faster payouts, cashback, and invitations to special events.
Volatility
See "Slot Volatility (Variance)" above. Volatility applies to all casino games and indicates the level of risk and the frequency and size of potential payouts.

W

Wagering Requirements (Playthrough)
The number of times you must bet through a bonus amount before you can withdraw the winnings. For example, a $100 bonus with 30x wagering requirements means you must place a total of $3,000 in bets before cashing out. Lower is better for players.
Whale
Casino industry term for a very high roller who wagers extremely large sums. Whales are the most valuable customers and receive the highest level of VIP treatment.
Wild Symbol
A special symbol in slot games that substitutes for other symbols (except scatters and bonus symbols) to help complete winning combinations. Variations include expanding wilds, sticky wilds, and stacked wilds.
Withdrawal
The process of transferring winnings from your casino account to your personal bank, e-wallet, or crypto wallet. Processing times vary by method — crypto is typically fastest (minutes to hours), while bank transfers may take 3-5 business days.
