How We Review Casinos

Every casino goes through our 6-point evaluation before it appears on Winning VIPs.

1
License Verification
We confirm the casino holds an active license and check regulatory records for complaints or sanctions.
2
Bonus Analysis
We break down wagering requirements, max bet limits, game weighting, and expiry dates for every promotion.
3
Game Library Audit
We catalog providers, count titles, test RTP claims, and check for certified RNG systems.
4
Deposit & Withdrawal Test
We make real deposits and withdrawals to measure processing times, fees, and minimum/maximum limits.
5
Support Quality
We test live chat, email, and phone support at different hours to evaluate response time and helpfulness.
6
Ongoing Monitoring
Ratings aren't permanent. We re-evaluate casinos quarterly and update scores when terms, ownership, or quality changes.
